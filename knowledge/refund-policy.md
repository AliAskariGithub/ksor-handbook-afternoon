Customers may return items within 30 days of purchase with proof of receipt. Refunds are processed within 5 working days of receiving the returned item.

## Return Conditions

Items must be unused and in their original condition to qualify for a refund. Original packaging should be included where possible.

## Receipt Requirement

A valid receipt or proof of purchase is required for all returns. This can be:

- Original paper receipt
- Email confirmation
- Order number from online purchase

## Processing Time

Once we receive your returned item, refunds are processed and credited back to your original payment method within 5 working days.

## Faulty Goods Exception

Faulty or defective goods are exempt from the 30-day return window. If an item develops a fault after the standard return period, customers should contact us immediately. Faulty goods will be assessed and replaced or refunded regardless of the time elapsed since purchase.

## How to Return

To initiate a return:

1. Contact customer service with your receipt and order details
2. Pack the item securely in its original packaging if available
3. Return the item using the provided return label or instructions
4. Retain proof of postage until your refund is confirmed
